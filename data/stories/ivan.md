Ivan Matveevich Sokolov was born on 14 January 1898 in the village of Peski, in the family of the carpenter Matvey Ivanov Sokolov; the next day he was baptised in St Elijah church of the village of Kesova Gora [C019][C020][R019]. Like his father, he was a carpenter [C023].

With Anna Egorovna he had two children, Maria and Nikolay; Nikolay was born in Kashin in February 1931 [C026][C006][R011][R012].

In August 1941 Ivan Matveevich was called up by the Kashin district military office, and in 1942 he was killed on the Volkhov Front [C022][C021]. The Book of Remembrance gives 1897 as his year of birth, which disagrees with the parish register [X01].
