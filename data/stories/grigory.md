Grigory Vasilyevich Vershinin was born in 1872 in the family of a sexton, and in 1898 he was ordained priest to the Intercession church of Uglich [C033][C034]. His hagiography mentions a son, Alexey [R018].

In the autumn of 1937 Father Grigory was arrested; on 15 November he was sentenced to be shot, and on 20 November he was shot [C035][C036][C037]. He was rehabilitated in 1989 [C038], and in 2000, in this demo, he was glorified among the New Martyrs [C039].
